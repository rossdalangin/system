# Agency Nexus User Manual: Calibrate Your Operations

## 1. Initial Setup
1.  **Installation:** Install and activate the plugin in your WordPress dashboard.
2.  **Branding:** Go to **Agency Nexus > Settings**. Upload your logo and set your default hourly rate.
3.  **Communication:** Configure SMTP settings to ensure all system emails (invoices, alerts) are delivered reliably.

## 2. Lead Management (EngageTrack)
1.  **Capture:** Generate an embed code from the EngageTrack menu and place it on your contact page.
2.  **Nurture:** Click "Email Lead" to send follow-ups using your **Canned Responses**.
3.  **Conversion:** When a lead is ready to start, click **Convert to Client**. This creates a client record automatically.

## 3. Project Management & Tasks
1.  **Creation:** Create a new project or use the **SmartOnboard** scope builder to generate one.
2.  **Task Management:**
    *   Add tasks to projects.
    *   Click **Edit** on any task to add specific instructions or a detailed description.
    *   Use the **Delete** action to remove unnecessary tasks.
3.  **Time Tracking:** Team members can log hours against specific tasks.
4.  **Log Management:** In the Task Edit view, you can view, edit, or delete individual time logs to ensure your profitability data is accurate.

## 4. Financials (MoneyFlow)
1.  **Invoicing:** Generate branded invoices from the project view.
2.  **Profitability:** The dashboard widget and project views show your **True Profit** based on: `Budget - (Hours * Rate) - Expenses`.
3.  **Payments:** Enable Stripe or PayPal in settings to accept instant payments from the client portal.

## 5. Automation (AutoPilot)
1.  **Rules:** Create "If-This-Then-That" rules (e.g., *If Project Completed -> Trigger Zapier*).
2.  **Efficiency:** Use these rules to automate client onboarding and reporting.

## 6. Team Health (BurnoutGuard)
1.  **Check-ins:** Encourage team members to log their stress levels via the health dashboard.
2.  **Capacity:** Monitor assigned tasks vs. capacity to prevent burnout and maintain quality.
