# Agency Nexus User Manual: Calibrate Your Operations

## 1. Initial Setup
1.  **Installation:** Install and activate the plugin in your WordPress dashboard.
2.  **Branding:** Go to **Agency Nexus > Settings**. Upload your logo and set your default hourly rate.
3.  **Communication:** Configure SMTP settings to ensure all system emails (invoices, alerts) are delivered reliably.

## 2. Lead Management & CRM (EngageTrack)
1.  **Capture:** Generate an embed code from the EngageTrack menu and place it on your contact page.
2.  **Scoring:** Every lead is automatically scored (0-100) based on their source and potential value. Focus on high-score leads first.
3.  **Social Hub:** Monitor engagement across platforms in the Unified Social Dashboard. Priority interactions are highlighted for immediate reply.
4.  **Nurture:** Click "Email Lead" to send follow-ups using your **Canned Responses**.
5.  **Conversion:** When a lead is ready to start, click **Convert to Client**. This creates a client record automatically.

## 3. Onboarding & Proposals (SmartOnboard)
1.  **Scope Building:** Use the Interactive Scope Builder to standardize your service packages.
2.  **Proposals:** After building a scope, a Proposal is generated. Send the link to your client.
3.  **Acceptance:** Clients can review and e-sign proposals in their portal. Accepted proposals are automatically converted into Projects.

## 4. Project Management & Tasks
1.  **Creation:** Managed through the Projects menu.
2.  **Task Management:**
    *   Add tasks to projects.
    *   **Dependencies:** Link tasks using the "Depends On" field to manage execution flow.
    *   **Instructions:** Click **Edit** on any task to add specific instructions or a detailed description.
3.  **Time Tracking:** Team members can log hours against specific tasks.
4.  **Buffer Management:** Set "Buffer Days" in Project settings to account for risk and scope creep.

## 5. Content Operations (ContentMatrix)
1.  **Pillar Architect:** Plan your content in topic clusters. Mark high-level pieces as "Pillars" and link "Cluster" content to them.
2.  **Forecasting:** Every content piece includes an engagement forecast based on platform strength and pillar relevance.
3.  **Batching:** Use the Batch Automation tool to generate multiple drafts for a project in seconds.

## 6. Financials (MoneyFlow)
1.  **Invoicing:** Generate branded invoices from the project view.
2.  **Profitability:** The dashboard widget and project views show your **True Profit** based on: `Budget - (Hours * Rate) - Expenses`.
3.  **Payments:** Enable Stripe or PayPal in settings to accept instant payments from the client portal.

## 7. Client Collaboration (ClientSync)
1.  **Messaging:** Secure, real-time chat with clients.
2.  **Meetings:** Use the Meeting Scheduler to coordinate strategy calls across timezones.
3.  **Boundary Enforcement:** Set your office hours in Settings. The system will automatically send an after-hours response to clients messaging you outside those times.

## 8. Productivity & Time Management (TimeBlock Pro)
1.  **Scheduling:** Block out Deep Work vs. Shallow Work periods in your calendar.
2.  **Focus Mode:** Use the "Focus Mode" timer to eliminate distractions during deep work sessions.
3.  **Analytics:** Use **Productivity Reports** to see your "Focus Intensity" and time allocation over the last 30 days.

## 9. Security & Compliance
1.  **Encryption:** Enable "Encrypt internal notes at rest" in **Security & Privacy** to protect sensitive client data.
2.  **GDPR:** Use the Data Portability tool to export a full JSON package of any client's data upon request.

## 10. Automation (AutoPilot)
1.  **Rules:** Create "If-This-Then-That" rules (e.g., *If Project Completed -> Trigger Zapier*).
2.  **Daily Briefing:** The system sends a daily summary email to the admin covering overdue tasks, new leads, and urgent social activity.

## 11. Team Health (BurnoutGuard)
1.  **Check-ins:** Encourage team members to log their stress levels via the health dashboard.
2.  **Vacations:** Plan your OOO time in the Vacation Planner to automatically adjust your availability and capacity metrics.
3.  **Capacity:** Monitor assigned tasks vs. capacity to prevent burnout and maintain quality.
