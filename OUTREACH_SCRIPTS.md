# Outreach Scripts: Master the Sale with Agency Nexus

These scripts are designed for different stages of the sales cycle, specifically for agency owners selling "Agency Nexus" as a service or a product.

---

## 1. Warm Outreach (Existing Peer/Network)
**Scenario:** You’re talking to a fellow agency owner who is clearly overwhelmed.

"Hey {{Name}}, I’ve been watching you scale lately—congrats on the growth! One thing I’ve noticed with a lot of my peers is that 'Tool Chaos' starts to bite hard once you hit a certain volume.

I recently moved my entire HQ to **Agency Nexus** (it’s an all-in-one OS built on WordPress). It pulled our PM, Invoices, and Client Chat into one branded dashboard. It saved my team about 10 hours of admin this week alone. Might be worth a look if you’re tired of jumping between Slack and Asana."

---

## 2. Cold Outreach (Targeted Prospects)
**Scenario:** Reaching out to a WordPress-based agency that shows signs of operational friction.

"Hi {{Name}},

I was looking at your site and noticed a few operational gaps that we typically help agencies solve—specifically around client onboarding speed and ROI tracking.

Most agencies lose 20% of their margin to 'Uncalibrated' operations. We use a system called **Agency Nexus** that allows us to onboard clients in 60 seconds with a professional scope builder and branded portal.

Would you be open to a 10-minute chat about how 'calibrating' your workflow could increase your project margins by 15-20%?"

---

## 3. Partnership Outreach (Hosting / SaaS / Themes)
**Scenario:** Seeking affiliates or bundle partners.

"Hello {{Partner_Team}},

I’m the creator of **Agency Nexus**, the first comprehensive OS for agencies built directly on WordPress.

I’ve noticed that many of your users are freelancers struggling with fragmented management tools. I’d love to discuss a potential affiliate partnership or a bundle offer.

Agency Nexus adds massive 'stickiness' to your hosting/theme ecosystem by turning a simple WordPress site into a professional service-delivery engine. Interested in a quick demo?"

---

## 4. Closing the Deal (Post-Discovery Call)
**Scenario:** Moving a lead to the scope/proposal stage.

"Great chatting today, {{Name}}!

I’ve gone ahead and built your project roadmap in our **SmartOnboard** builder. You should have received a link to your secure Client Portal.

In the portal, you can:
1. Review and e-sign the Proposal.
2. View our interactive Gantt chart.
3. Settle the initial deposit via Stripe/PayPal.

We believe in total transparency, so once we start, you'll be able to track our progress in real-time through that same dashboard. Looking forward to working with you!"

---

## 5. Reactivating "Lost" Leads
**Scenario:** A prospect went cold 3 months ago.

"Hi {{Name}},

I was just reviewing our project ROI reports in our **Nexus dashboard** and your name came up.

Last we spoke, you were looking to scale your [Service]. We’ve just upgraded our entire delivery system to a more 'Calibrated' model that has cut our turnaround times by 30%.

If you're still looking for results without the operational headache, I’d love to show you the new workflow. Are you free for 5 minutes this Thursday?"
